The Problem

Security systems watch. They don't understand.

Cameras and microphones capture more footage than any human can meaningfully review — and by the time a threat is visually obvious, the moment to act on it has often already passed.

01

Footage without interpretation

Raw video and audio streams record everything but understand nothing. Intent, stress, and escalation are invisible to a passive feed.

02

Operators can't watch everything

One person monitoring dozens of feeds will miss the subtle signal — a raised voice, a flash of fear — buried in hours of routine footage.

03

Detection arrives too late

By the time an incident is visually unmistakable, the window for calm de-escalation or early intervention has usually already closed.

How It Works

Feed to alert in under 400 milliseconds.

A continuous four-stage pipeline runs against every connected stream.

Under the Hood

Three neural networks. One DSP engine. One fusion formula.

Facial Pipeline — Neural Networks

tinyFaceDetectorFace detection, Tiny-YOLO style
faceLandmark68TinyNet68-point facial landmarks
faceExpressionNet7-class expression softmax

Vocal Pipeline — Signal Processing

Autocorrelation F0Pitch detection, 60–400Hz
RMS / Jitter / RateLoudness, stability, pace

Threat Fusion Formula

facial = weighted(angry, fearful, disgusted…)
fused = 0.65×facial + 0.35×vocal + lexicon
index ← index + (fused − index) × 0.12

LOW < 35 ≤ ELEVATED < 65 ≤ HIGH

All weights and thresholds are operator-tunable in the console's settings drawer — nothing is hardcoded.
